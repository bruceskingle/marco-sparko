# Using the Graphs

Most pages with readings can show them three ways, chosen with the buttons at the top:

- **Table**, the figures for each period.
- **Graph**, a bar for each period.
- **Trend**, a line of the daily totals, with a line for each year so that they can be compared.

## Periods

Buttons such as **30min**, **Day**, **Week** and **Month** choose how long each bar is. Short periods over a long range would be too many bars to draw, so those buttons are disabled, and say *Zoom in to see more*: choose a shorter range first.

## Zooming

- **Drag across bars** to zoom in to them.
- **Double click a bar** to see it in more detail, with shorter bars.
- **Right click** for a menu: **Zoom Out** goes back to where you were before zooming in, **Previous** and **Next** move to the range before or after, and **Reload this bar** downloads its data again.

Hold the mouse over a bar to see its figures.

## Categories

Where the bars are split into parts, such as rates or kinds of charging, ![Categories](icons/categories.svg) **Categories** lists them. Each can be hidden, or given a different colour, and it shows the total of each.

## Trend

**Smoothing** makes the trend easier to read: **7 day** and **30 day** show the average of the days around each one, and **Cumulative** adds them up through each year.

## Export and Reload

![Export](icons/export.svg) **Export** saves what is shown as a CSV file, which spreadsheets such as Excel open, or as JSON. ![Reload](icons/reload.svg) **Reload**, where there is one, downloads everything shown again from the supplier, as far as it still has it, overwriting the cached values.

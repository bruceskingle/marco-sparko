# Choosing Dates

Pages with readings or sessions show those in a range of dates, chosen at the top of the page.

- ![Previous](icons/previous.svg) and ![Next](icons/next.svg) move to the same length of time before or after.
- ![Change the period](icons/calendar.svg) and the dates shown opens the date picker, where **From** and **To** set the start and end, each with a date and a half hour. Its quick buttons choose a common range: **This Week**, **7 Days**, **This Month** and **30 Days** end today, and **Last Week**, **Prev 7 Days**, **Last Month** and **Prev 30 Days** are the ones before them.

Data is downloaded from the supplier the first time a range is shown, and saved, so it is quick to show again. Data which may still change, such as recent readings, is checked again from time to time.

Dragging across a [graph](graphs.md) also zooms in to a shorter range.

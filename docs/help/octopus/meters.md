# Meters

A summary of the meters and devices on your account.

## Meter Points

Each electricity meter point, by its MPAN, whether it is **import** or **export**, and the product code of its tariff. Click an MPAN to see [its readings](meter.md), half hourly, daily, weekly or monthly, with their cost.

## Smart Device Network

The devices on your smart meter network. Where there is a Home Mini, a link shows [its readings every 10 seconds](home-mini.md).

## Smart Flex Devices

Devices Octopus can control, such as an EV charger on an Intelligent tariff. Click one to see [its charging sessions](charging-sessions.md).

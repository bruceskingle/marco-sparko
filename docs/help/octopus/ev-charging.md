# EV Charging

Your EV chargers known to Octopus, such as one on an Intelligent tariff, with the car Octopus has configured for it: its make, model and battery size, and the charger's power.

Octopus does not know which car is actually charging, only the one configured, so figures based on it, such as the percentage of the battery added, are only right for that car.

Click a charger to see [its charging sessions](charging-sessions.md).

# Bills

Every bill on your account, the newest first. Click a bill's reference to open it in [a window of its own](bill.md), which leaves this list where it was. Several bills can be open at once, to compare them, and the **Window** menu lists them, can bring any of them to the front, arrange them side by side, or close them.

The columns are the date the bill was issued, its reference, the period it covers and its type, then the balance brought forward, the charges and credits with their tax, and the balance carried forward.

A bill without an issue date is still **open**: Octopus is still adding to it, it is not shown in the Octopus app yet, and it may change or be replaced before it is issued.

Bills which were downloaded this time are shown differently from those which were already saved.

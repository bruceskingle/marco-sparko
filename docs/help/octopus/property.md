# A Property

## Meters

The property's electricity and gas meters, with their MPAN (electricity) or MPRN (gas), serial number, whether they are active, and whether they provide half hourly readings. Click a meter to see [its readings](meter.md).

An electricity meter is either **import**, measuring what you take from the grid, or **export**, measuring what you send back, e.g. from solar panels or a battery.

## Smart Device Network

The devices on the property's smart meter network, such as the meters themselves and the in home display. A meter with a **Home Mini**, or another consumer access device, has its readings every 10 seconds, see [the Home Mini](home-mini.md).

# Help

Marco Sparko shows the data your energy supplier holds about you, such as bills, meter readings and charging sessions, and keeps a copy on your computer so that it is still there after the supplier stops providing it.

The help panel shows help about the screen you are looking at. Links like these open more detailed pages, and ![Back](icons/back.svg) at the top returns to where you were. ![Close](icons/close.svg) hides the help, and ![Help](icons/help.svg) at the top right of the window shows it again.
